# Exact collective inclusion transfer

Use this only when the quantitative threshold, labeled underlying degrees,
complete live integer exterior contract and future-edge legality agree.

1. Obtain a genuinely exact positive comparison against an intermediate
   family. UNKNOWN, solver timeout and an ancestor separator are not positive
   evidence against or for the final family.
2. Bind source, feedback, implementation and certificate hashes. Rederive the
   actual operation and its boundary permutation; never relabel a feedback
   hash to manufacture an earlier failed query.
3. Check intermediate representatives' actual states, constructions and
   vertex counts. Each whole intermediate member must remain genuinely
   retained or have independently verified coverage by the final family.
   Producer deletion flags alone do not establish final-family inclusion.
4. Compose degree-preserving boundary maps and mapped future-edge subset
   gates. The same unrestricted exterior domain must survive every
   replacement, including empty and private closed exteriors.
5. At fixed degrees, equal complete live capped profiles and inclusion of
   eligible-edge sets permit reuse of a previously proved coverage fact.
   Cache inclusion facts, not virtual representatives.
6. Pure genuine insertion preserves previous positive proofs. Still account
   for every new unary obligation and every required proper relative order
   on the fixed final family before asserting closure. Bind the final
   snapshot and keep intermediate and final certificate scopes distinct.
7. An independent literal checker may choose a different canonical boundary
   order. Supply the actual feedback permutation explicitly when available,
   record its origin and map exterior markers through that permutation.
   Compare full routing counts and the required exact completion coefficient,
   not just the graph order or an apparently matching marker number.
8. Derive a resumed unary cursor by summing legal specifications over the
   actual retained rows in their published order and subtracting the proved
   prefix. A producer cursor or queue position is not a proof frontier.
   Count every appended graph's entire new unary schedule. A larger proved
   prefix can coexist with more unfinished obligations, so compare the
   actual covered count with the final specification sum.

An exact transferred case is a mathematical improvement over UNKNOWN, not
by itself a theorem, counterexample, publication result or performance claim.
