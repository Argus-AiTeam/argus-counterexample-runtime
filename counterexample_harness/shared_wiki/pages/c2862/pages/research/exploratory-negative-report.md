# Campaign 2862 exploratory negative boundary

holonomy into `SU(2)`, the complete second-Chern Chern--Simons class in
`R/Z`, and a proof that the class is not in `Q/Z`. No exact irrational value
and no candidate have been produced.

Other nonperiodic monodromies, other seed placements, and closures introducing
new holonomy remain open. These results neither prove universal rationality
nor exclude all topology-first constructions, and they make no novelty claim.
