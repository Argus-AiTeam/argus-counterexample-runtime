# Closed-surface noncentral connector rank-one boundary

For

```text
F=D_(b^-1 c) o (phi^3 union psi^3),
```

the reviewed quartic rank-one block cannot be continued to a noncentral
connector by conjugating and centrally signing its mirror second handle.
With

```text
p(x)=x^4+x^3-2*x^2+1,
C4(x)=x^4-4*x^2+2,
```

the four sign choices are imposed on the exact unrestricted fixed-character
ideal by

```text
Ta=x, Tb=Tab=C4(x),
Tc=epsilon_c*C4(x), Td=epsilon_d*x,
Tcd=epsilon_c*epsilon_d*C4(x),
zz*((Tb*Tc-Tbc)^2-4)=1.
```

All four characteristic-zero systems have unit ideal.  The trace equations
contain every arbitrarily conjugated signed mirror pair, and no stable
intertwiner is fixed in advance.  Thus this ansatz contains no noncentral
complex fixed character, hence no irreducible `SU(2)` candidate.

This does not classify the unrestricted affine-rank-one locus.  A different
point can have unrelated exponent and root-of-unity data and need not lie in
the signed-mirror trace slice.  No finite exponent bound is part of the
problem, so bounded enumeration cannot close that gap.  The complete
mapping-cone phase is therefore not evaluated on this empty slice, and no
candidate is emitted.
