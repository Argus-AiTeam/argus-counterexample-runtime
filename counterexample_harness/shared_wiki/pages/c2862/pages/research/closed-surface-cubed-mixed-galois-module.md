# Closed-surface cubed-mixed Galois module

For `u=r^2`, the cubic field is

```text
K=Q(u),   u^3-4u^2+5u-1=0,   disc(K polynomial)=-23.
```

The degree-48 coefficient field has the multiquadratic presentation

```text
F=K(i,r,t,q),
i^2=-1, r^2=u, t^2=u-4, q^2=3u-2.
```

If `N` is the degree-six `S_3` splitting field of the cubic and
`u_0,u_1,u_2` are its roots, then

```text
L=N(i,r_0,r_1,r_2,t_0,t_1,t_2)
```

is the normal closure of `F`.  Its six Kummer square classes are
independent, so `[L:Q]=384` and

```text
Gal(L/Q) = (C2 x C2^2 x C2^3) semidirect S3.
```

Conditional on the reviewed identification of the 205-term good cycle
with the complete 194-term mapping-cone class, directed Borel balls
prove that no Galois element moving the physical cubic root to a
noncompact root sends `beta` to `+beta` or `-beta` modulo torsion.  Thus
the line generated by `beta` is not a cross-block trivial or sign line.

The physical block is exactly zero, rather than only numerically close
to zero.  The real cubic root lies in `(6/25,1/4)`, where the exact
quaternion Gram matrix is positive definite and all four `s` roots are
real.  These eight embedding pairs are compact `PSU(2)`
representations, and the real Borel class restricts to zero on
`PSU(2)`.

The full regulator module also excludes a trivial constituent:

```text
(B(L) tensor Q)^Gal(L/Q)=0.
```

A fixed regulator vector would be constant on all embeddings, whereas
complex conjugation negates it.  Neumann--Yang injectivity modulo
torsion and Maschke semisimplicity then force it to vanish.


is the odd transposition fixing `u`.  Although inversion relations
alone leave an exact 166-coordinate formal difference, an exact
mapping-torus reversor settles its Bloch action.  On the free surface


Thus `A([x,v])=[X(x),1-v]` reverses both fiber and base and preserves
the mapping-torus orientation.  One explicit nonzero matrix `P`

Bloch symbol termwise.

conjugate to the original representation.  Hence the full stabilizer of

```text

multiplicity(trivial)=multiplicity(sign)=0.
```
