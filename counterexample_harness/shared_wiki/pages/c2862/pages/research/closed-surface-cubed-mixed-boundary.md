# Closed-surface cubed mixed fixed-point boundary

For

```text
F=D_gamma o (phi^3 union psi^3),       gamma=b^-1 c,
```

the specified roots of

```text
r^6-4r^4+5r^2-1=0
```

and the recorded `s` branch give an exact rank-three irreducible
`SU(2)` fixed representation.  Its `gamma` holonomy is noncentral, and
the exact stable intertwiner implements all four mapping relations.

In the second-Chern normalization

```text
CS_+=(1/(8*pi^2))*integral tr(A dA+(2/3)A^3) in R/Z,
```

the thirteen calibrated local Dehn-twist terms reduce to

```text
a^2/2-2a+b^2/2+b-(g^2+h^2+k^2)/4+17/16.
```

The five base eigenvalues are algebraic and non-torsion, so each
normalized angle is transcendental by Gelfond--Schneider.  This does
not prove the multivariable quadratic expression irrational.

The stable matrix has the exact word form

```text
U=-rho(c^-1 b c^-1).
```

After the corresponding based change, the stable generator can be
made central, but the mapping-torus class still maps to the secondary
bar cycle `[q_#e]`, where

```text
partial e=(F_#-1)z
```

for a surface fundamental bar cycle `z`.  The stable word identity
shows that this image is closed; it does not show that it is a boundary
or torsion.  An explicit based surface gauge with a Wess--Zumino
extension, or the equivalent labelled mapping-torus bar cycle, is still
required for the full phase.

Accordingly the exact fixed point is retained, but its full
Chern--Simons class and irrationality remain open.  No counterexample
candidate is emitted from the local phase.
