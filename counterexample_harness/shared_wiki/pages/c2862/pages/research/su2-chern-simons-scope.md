# SU(2) Chern--Simons source boundary

Auckly 1994 uses `Sp(1)=SU(2)` and `cs in R/Z`.  Printed p. 235 proposes
irreducible representations on figure-eight `-1/2` surgery only as possible
**non-component-defined** representations; it gives no value, branch, or
irrationality claim.  Printed p. 251 still asks whether all Chern--Simons
invariants are rational.

Kirk--Klassen 1990, printed pp. 360--363, fix a preferred longitude and the
extension relation `mu^p lambda^q=1`.  Their `-1/2` table has four `SU(2)`
rows and only six-decimal `cs` values.  Their p. 364 values `0.02804` and
`0.26702` belong to `-1/4` surgery and an `SO(3)`-normalized minimum, not a
50-digit `SU(2)` value.

Campaign 2862 fixes the word-level group and four exact algebraic `SU(2)`
representations in `research/SCOPE_SPECIFICATION.md`.  No primary source for
the alleged 50-digit datum or unique source-selected branch is known.  Exact
fixed-path regulator and principal-dilogarithm formulas for all four branches
are now recorded in `research/FOUR_BRANCH_PERIODS.md`, but no applicable
unconditional theorem proves any one of them irrational modulo integers.
They therefore remain exact test cases, not counterexamples.
