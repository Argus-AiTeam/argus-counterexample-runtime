# Infinite-rank automorphic template splitting

On the C34 rational four-ary packing, let

\[
P_\tau(z)=\sum_{k\geq1}4^{-k}\sum_{|w|=k}
\tau_w\frac{s_k}{z-c_w},\qquad |\tau_w|\leq1,
\]

and set

\[
F_\tau=(z,z-1+P_\tau/100).
\]

For the all-one coefficient array, the normalized principal-part mass is
one on every level and the second coordinate has a genuine pole at every
deleted center. Thus this member is nonextendable across every hole.

An exact lattice-shell estimate gives

\[
\sum_{|w|=k}\frac{s_k}{|z-c_w|}\leq25\cdot2^k.
\]

After multiplication by \(4^{-k}\), the level bounds are
\(25\cdot2^{-k}\). Consequently

\[
\|P_\tau\|_\infty\leq25,\qquad
\max(|z|,|z-1+P_\tau/100|)\geq\frac14.
\]

A compact-support Cauchy-transform correction gives an exact bounded
Bezout row on every finite truncation and on the limiting domain:

\[
zG_\tau+(z-1+P_\tau/100)H_\tau=1,\qquad
\|G_\tau\|_\infty\leq216,\quad
\|H_\tau\|_\infty\leq130.
\]

For a universal cover and the convention

\[
R\circ\gamma-R=a_\gamma(-F_2,F_1),
\]

the cocycle identity is

\[
a_{\gamma\eta}=a_\gamma\circ\eta+a_\eta.
\]

The invariant pullback of the global row implies, for every bounded disk
row, that

\[
a_\gamma=b\circ\gamma-b
\]

with bounded analytic \(b\). Hence the row-independent connecting class
vanishes throughout this frozen template.

This boundary does not cover arbitrary pairs on the C34 domain or
arbitrary bounded analytic deck-group cocycles.
