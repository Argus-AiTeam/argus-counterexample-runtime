# Kneser log-slope self-sampling boundary

Let \(a=A_K'\) be named on \((-1,\infty)\) and totalized by zero outside.
The fixed guarded family

\[
x>-1,\qquad
y=c+\frac{a'(x)}{a(x)}>-1,\qquad
a(y)=\lambda a(x),\quad \lambda>0,
\]

uses only \(a\) and its first-order definable derivative. It contains no
Abel-function symbol, inverse, iterate index, or restricted analytic symbol.

For the genuine positive Kneser derivative \(g=A_K'\), put

\[
q=\log g,\qquad \ell=-q'.
\]

The family is exactly the level equation

\[
F_c(x)=q(c-\ell(x))-q(x)=\log\lambda,
\]

and

\[
F_c'(x)=\ell(x)+\ell(c-\ell(x))\ell'(x).
\]

The reviewed Kneser tail estimates

\[
\ell(x)\geq\frac1{2x},\qquad
|\ell'(x)|\leq\frac C{x^2},\qquad
\sup_{t>-1}|\ell(t)|=B<\infty
\]

make \(F_c'>0\) on one physical upper tail independent of \(c\). On the
compact core, bounded \(c\) gives one fixed restricted-analytic family.
As \(c\to+\infty\), \(F_c'=\ell+E_c\) with
\(E_c\to0\) in every finite \(C^r\) norm, so finite-jet stability bounds
all critical points uniformly. Rolle's theorem then gives one finite global
root bound for every shared \((c,\lambda)\).

This excludes only the displayed self-sampling family. It does not bound
other formulas in \((\mathbb R_{\exp},a)\), and it leaves both the
fixed-formula unbounded-fibers claim and the unrestricted campaign claim
open.
