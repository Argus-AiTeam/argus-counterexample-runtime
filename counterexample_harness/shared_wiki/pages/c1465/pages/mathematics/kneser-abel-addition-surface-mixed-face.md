# Kneser Abel addition-surface mixed-face bound

For the frozen surface
\[
A(z)=A(x)+A(y)
\]
in Abel coordinates \(x=T(s)\), \(y=T(t)\), \(z=T(s+t)\), every normal
incidence has the exact common-level form
\[
K=T'(s)(a-T(s))
 =T'(t)(b-T(t))
 =-T'(s+t)(c-T(s+t)).
\]

On the complete mixed face \(0<s\le V<t\), use
\[
\sigma=\log\frac{T'(t+s)}{T'(t)},\quad
\lambda=\frac{|K|}{T'(t)T(t)},\quad
\chi=\frac{|K|}{T'(t+s)T(t+s)}.
\]
The lower projective scales are
\[
\mu=\frac{|a-T(s)|}{T(t)},\qquad
\tau=\frac{|a-T(s)|}{T(t+s)}.
\]
These coordinates exhaust fixed-positive \(s\), all microscopic
\(\sigma\) limits, both center cancellations, and every finite or
projective-center face.

On each compact \(s\)-profile branch, the addition residual satisfies
\[
\mathscr R'
=-\frac{\det H_q}{h_sh_th_u},
\qquad
\det H_q=h_sh_t+h_sh_u+h_th_u.
\]
Every escaping positive focal point is forced into
\[
\frac{K L(t)}{T'(t)^2}\to1,
\]
and every escaping negative focal point is forced into
\[
\frac{(-K)L(t+s)}{T'(t+s)^2}\to1.
\]
Exact differentiation along the corresponding inverse branches makes all
focal zeros on one branch cross in the same direction. Hence each open
branch has at most one focal point and at most two residual roots.
Compact collars, profile births, simultaneous births, seams, and interval
fibers have one uniform finite bound.

The microscopic finite-\(\sigma\) leading center map has determinant
\[
(1-\kappa)(r^2+\kappa),
\qquad
\kappa=\frac{K}{T'(t)T(t)},\quad
r=\frac{T'(t+s)}{T'(t)}.
\]
Its only leading degeneracies are the same two profile-birth tubes; no
independent return phase survives the blowup.

Combining both mixed orientations with the reviewed compact-core bound and
double-high eleven-point theorem gives one finite component bound for every
fiber of the literal Abel addition-surface formula. Thus this frozen
formula cannot realize arbitrary shared-center multiplicity or
`Alt_M`. This is a formula-level negative result only: the existence of
another fixed formula with unbounded fibers and the unrestricted
o-minimality claim remain open.
