# Endpoint-notch central-orbit transport boundary

The exact controlled-quotient seed has 772 distinct nonzero frame
occurrences, uses scales \(1,2,4,8,16\), and has eight records based at
the identity. Its complete two-bank six-cycle is covariant under left
translation, while its left-generator frame occurrences are covariant
under right translation.

For a horizontal translation \(q=X(U,V,C)\), the three right-translated
left-label pair defects are multiples of \(3V\), and the three
right-label pair defects are multiples of \(3U\). Conversely,
left-translated \(a^r\)- and \(b^r\)-frame edges acquire central defects
\(rV\) and \(-rU\). Thus a translation preserves both the bank cycle and
both frame families exactly only when \(U=V=0\).

Dyadic dilation preserves both structures and allows at most \(K_m-4\)
placements of the five seed scales. A genuine central translate must
keep the translated identity basepoint in \(I_m\), so there are at most
\(2h_m^2+1\) central roots. Therefore the union of every allowed direct
orbit has at most
\[
772(K_m-4)(2h_m^2+1)
\]
frame occurrences. Combining this with
\(|I_m|\geq h_m^4/2^{13}\) and the exact support estimate gives
\[
\Gamma_m(y)<\frac{3080}{h_m}\|y\|_2
\]
for every coefficient vector supported on that union, with arbitrary
coefficients and overlaps.

This rules out direct symmetry periodization even though it uses a
positive fraction of the scale indices. It does not cover noncentral
roots equipped with root-dependent corrective flows, so the unrestricted
complete transport-gap dichotomy remains open.
