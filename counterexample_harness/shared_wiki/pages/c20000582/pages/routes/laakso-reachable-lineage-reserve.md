---

At the genuine reachable \(k=1\) contact
\((53,51,55)\), the complete joint spectral measure has 73 atoms in
eight minimal-factor birth families.  Keeping each family's full
\(2\times2\) near/far projector matrix and all four old children gives
exact descendant counts
\[
73,\ 292,\ 1168,\ 4672,\ 18688
\]
through old horizon four.

The complete-family subtree positivity proposal is false.  For every
\(r=0,\ldots,4\), factor 2 satisfies
\[
-1/5{,}000{,}000<L_{2,r}<-1/6{,}000{,}000,
\]
and factor 6 satisfies
\[
-1/3{,}500{,}000<L_{6,r}<-1/4{,}500{,}000.
\]
These bounds were obtained both from a fresh exact graph,
Sturm/residue, and old-child reconstruction and from an independent
replay of the reviewed rational atom table; all corresponding intervals
overlap.

The route does not produce a negative complete margin.  Factor 3 is
larger than \(1/400{,}000\) on every tested horizon and strictly pays the
two negative families using fractions \(2/25\) and \(4/35\).  Jacobian
returned the exact optimal total \(34/175<1\), leaving \(141/175\) of
that finite source bound.  The sum over all eight families remains
positive.

The five provenance channels were also retained: the direct and
root-00 channels are positive, the other three root channels are
negative, and their exact complete sum is positive.  Thus dropping
negative channels or cross correlations is invalid.

This is a reachable counterexample to an intermediate invariant, not to
the campaign objective.  No old-generator or digit-\(3\) preservation
theorem follows from the finite charge, no complete negative margin was
found, and the universal repeated-digit-\(3\) sign remains open.
