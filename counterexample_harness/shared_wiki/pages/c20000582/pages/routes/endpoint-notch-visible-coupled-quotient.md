# Endpoint-notch visible coupled-observation boundary

The minimal support of the central functional is the union
\(\Sigma_m\) of all nonloop central endpoint pairs.  The complete
four-side observation support is the larger connected set
\[
W_m=I_m\cup\{a^rx,xa^r,b^rx,xb^r:x\in I_m,\ r=2^j\}.
\]
On \(W_m\), the complete observation kernel consists only of constants.
Since constants are two-bank, the associated bank quotient has kernel
exactly the restricted two-bank space.  The previous outer-shell
six-cycle is therefore absent after the honest restriction.

With both left and right sides divided by the same exact profile
denominators, let \(\mathcal W_m\) be the full four-side observation
energy and
\[
\Lambda_m=\sup_{\mathcal W_m(R)>0}
\frac{\mathcal V_m(R)}{\mathcal W_m(R)}.
\]
This comparison is genuinely sufficient: the reviewed controlled
two-bank theorem and complete-pair one-Lipschitzness give
\[
\nu_m\leq
\left(\sqrt{56A_m}+\sqrt{2\Lambda_m}\right)^2,
\qquad
A_m=\frac{73728}{h_m}+\frac{169869312}{\sqrt m}.
\]
Thus \(\Lambda_m\to0\) would imply unrestricted scalar decay.

The full zero-extended Fourier operator retains
\(\widehat\chi_{u,v}(\phi-\theta)\) in every term and adds both generator
families.  It is one coupled Hermitian two-frequency form, not a direct
integral of independent frequencies.

An exact translated alternating six-cycle lies wholly in \(\Sigma_m\),
is complete-pair one-Lipschitz, and is not two-bank.  Direct physical
counting gives
\[
\frac{\mathcal V_m(g_m)}{\mathcal W_m(g_m)}=\frac12
\quad\text{for every }m\geq4.
\]
Hence \(\Lambda_m\geq1/2\), so this full quadratic observation comparison
cannot prove decay.  The same family satisfies
\[
\mathcal V_m(g_m)\leq\frac{25}{384m}\to0.
\]
It is therefore a mechanism no-go, not a positive-energy scalar family,
an unrestricted decay theorem, or the campaign counterexample.
