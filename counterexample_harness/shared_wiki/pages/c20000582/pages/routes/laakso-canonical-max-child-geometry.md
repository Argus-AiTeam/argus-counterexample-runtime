For the canonical all-zero-arm section of the unchanged depth-shifted Laakso
diffusion metric, every child endpoint state has an exact affine transfer in
the endpoint sum, difference, and imbalance resolvents \(S,D,X\). The transfer
retains both old endpoint rows, every \(p,b^0,b^1,q\) junction row, and the
complete direct killed term.

The frozen all-depth assertions
\[
a=c=\frac16,\qquad q=\frac35,\qquad B=2
\]
remain unresolved. They pass complete exact depth-one and depth-two audits,
targeted exact depth-three checks, and a floating depth-four falsification
screen, but no reachable invariant cone controls all four margin families.
There is no exact actual-depth failure of these frozen constants.

Two sharper constants do fail exactly at depth two under the complete schedule.
The cell \([0,4]\), child \([3,4]\), has squared chord ratio greater than
\(1/4\), so \(q=1/2\) is false. The subarc \([1,12]\) has diameter pair
\([1,10]\) with squared diameter-to-endpoint ratio greater than \(1\), so
global bounded turning with \(B=1\) is false.

Conditionally, any metric arc with the frozen child bounds, nonadjacent-child
separation, and global \(B=2\) bounded turning has every strict
\(R/16\)-packing in an \(R\)-ball bounded by
\[
M=2\cdot4^{14}=536870912.
\]
The reviewed orbit-submetry lift would then give full-space doubling constant
at most \(60M=32212254720\). These numbers are implications, not established
doubling bounds for the unchanged Laakso blocks.
