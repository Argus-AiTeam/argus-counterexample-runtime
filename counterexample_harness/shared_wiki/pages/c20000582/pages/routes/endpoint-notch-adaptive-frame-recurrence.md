# Endpoint-notch adaptive frame-recurrence boundary

For the frozen chain
\[
g_j=(2r16^j,\,2jr16^j,\,0),\qquad 0\leq j\leq k,
\]
every point has an explicit word of length at most one eighth of the
endpoint-notch word-ball radius. The slopes are all distinct, every
cross-scale group signature retains its symplectic term, and the outer
connector lies in the fixed window
\[
2^{-9}<\lambda_m|A-iW|<2^{-6}.
\]

For any normalized distortion-\(D\) map into \(\mathbb R^n\), the actual
shared-endpoint frame
\[
(u_j,c_j,a_j),\qquad a_ju_{j+1}=u_j+c_j,
\]
lies in \([-(26D+1),26D+1]^{2n+1}\). A rational net of radius \(1/(100D)\)
therefore gives an explicit finite recurrence depth.

This recurrence is compatible with all source distances. Radial lacunarity
forces \(|W|\leq(32/225)A\), so the complete endpoint-notch EDM is uniformly
comparable to the horizontal profile. The reviewed product horizontal atlas
then gives, at every depth, one complete EDM of centered rank at most ten:
\[
\Delta_k\leq E_k\leq30L_*^4\Delta_k.
\]
Hence actual frame recurrence and exact connector telescoping do not
contradict finite-dimensional embeddability for this frozen schema.

**Scope.** This is an all-depth boundary for one unbounded-slope but
radially lacunary adaptive chain. It does not embed the complete word balls,
exclude nonlacunary adaptive supports, or solve the Lang--Plaut problem.
