---
title: Repeated-digit-3 normalized total-gate Lyapunov boundary
description: Exact obstruction to a direct Schur/renewal Loewner proof, with no negative locator through scheduled depth nine.
---

For
\[
C_{k,r}=\sum_{j=0}^{k+r+2}96^j
[z^{2\cdot18^j}]A^rF_k,\qquad
M_{k,r}=16^{-(k+r+2)}C_{k,r},
\]
the two proposed total gates are exactly
\[
16^{k+r+3}(M_{k,r+1}-M_{k,r})
=C_{k,r+1}-16C_{k,r},
\]
\[
16^{k+r+2}(18M_{k+1,r}-M_{k,r})
=\frac98C_{k+1,r}-C_{k,r}.
\]

The complete digit-\(3\) Schur innovation is rank-one negative
semidefinite, but the signed near/far target reads an off-diagonal
entry. Such an entry has either sign even for rank-one negative
semidefinite matrices. On the actual profile, the first digit-\(3\)
defect starts with \( [z^6]=1/3888>0\) but is strictly negative at the
scheduled clock \(648\). Independently, the exact old-renewal error
satisfies
\[
E_{36}(1/4)>0>E_{36}(1/2).
\]
Thus Loewner order, atomwise squares, or pointwise renewal positivity
cannot by themselves prove either total gate. This does not exclude a
nonlinear phase-growing invariant that retains quantitative
cross-band reachability.

The reviewed five-channel recurrence and an independent
near/far/between joint reconstruction agree, including the corrected
65 source atoms and 257 first digit-\(3\) atoms. A 100-digit
pole-residue locator found all 28 margins and all 21 old gates positive
for \(k+r\le7\), and all digit-\(3\) gates through \(k=6\) positive.
Those finite perturbed-pole values are candidate screens only.

No all-\((k,r)\) gate theorem and no exact negative complete margin was
obtained. The direct Loewner/atomwise-square route is retired, while
the repeated-digit-\(3\) sign claim remains open.
