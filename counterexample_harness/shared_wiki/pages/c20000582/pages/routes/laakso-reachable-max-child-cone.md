For the frozen canonical max-child margin
\[
H_{w,j}=\frac9{25}P_w-C_{wj},
\]

commutes with horizontal reflection.  Thus
\[
H_{\bar w,3-j}=H_{w,j},
\]
which reduces the four child indices to two mirrored families but does not
identify the two children inside one asymmetric parent.

The cone requiring every scheduled clock prefix to be nonnegative contains the
phase-one root, but it is not closed under the actual child generators.  After
the genuine child-1 transition,
\[
[z^2]H_{1,0}=-\frac{11}{270},
\]
and this same first-clock value persists for every word \(1^k\), \(k\ge1\).
Nevertheless the complete phase-two scheduled margin is strictly positive.
Likewise, after one old shift of the outer-root margin, the first clock is
\(-184/75\), while later clocks compensate it and the complete margin remains
positive.  A negative individual clock is therefore not a campaign
counterexample.

Pointwise resolvent positivity fails on the same genuine repeated-interior
family:
\[
H_{1^k,0}(1)\le-\frac{32}{75}<0
\qquad(k\ge1).
\]
This excludes pointwise positivity as an invariant, not the scheduled
functional.

Finally, the exact old-dual degree law gives
\[
\deg \mathcal P(\lambda^n)=\frac n4
\quad(4\mid n),
\]
with nonzero leading coefficient.  The future scheduled pullbacks
\(\mathcal P^sQ_{m+s}\) therefore have pairwise distinct degrees, so no
uniformly fixed-rank faithful linear polynomial-test module can retain all
future old-prefix tests.

These results retire only clock-prefix positivity, pointwise resolvent
positivity, and uniformly fixed-rank faithful linear-test modules.  They do
not exclude nonlinear states, phase-growing representations, or quotients by
actual reachable measures.  No complete scheduled margin is negative, the
all-depth \(q=3/5\) assertion remains open, and the counterexample campaign
continues.
