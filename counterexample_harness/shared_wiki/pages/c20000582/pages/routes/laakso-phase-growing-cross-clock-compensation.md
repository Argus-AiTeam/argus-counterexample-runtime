title: Depth-shifted Laakso phase-growing cross-clock charge boundary
description: Exact root and reachable generator obstructions to two source-labelled charge rules; unrestricted flow reduces to the target suffix.
---

For the frozen max-child margin
\[
H_{w,j}=\frac9{25}P_w-C_{wj},
\]
retain every homogeneous root image and every complete \(S,X,D\)
direct-killed packet as a separate source.  At clock \(\nu\), its weighted
contribution is
\[
c_{\sigma,\nu}=96^\nu[z^{2\cdot18^\nu}]H^\sigma_{w,j}.
\]

A nonnegative conservation flow with only strictly later-to-earlier edges
cannot contain the root: every current candidate-direct packet has a
strictly negative terminal phase-one clock, although all four complete root
margins are positive.

Allowing generator-local same-clock payment only into the current direct
packet also fails exactly.  At the genuine state \(w=1\), candidate child
\(0\), the inherited digit-\(1\) direct packet has terminal contribution
\[
-\frac1{321}<c<-\frac1{322}<0,
\]
while the aggregate terminal clock lies between \(1/49\) and \(1/48\), all
aggregate clock suffixes are positive, and the complete scheduled margin is
strictly positive.

If same-clock redistribution is unrestricted, oriented nonnegative flow
exists exactly when every aggregate upper clock suffix is nonnegative.  The
lowest suffix is the desired complete scheduled margin itself.  A flow
assembled after evaluating that margin is therefore circular rather than a
generator-covariant certificate.

These facts retire only the two preregistered source-flow rules and post-hoc
unrestricted matching.  They do not prove or refute the all-depth \(q=3/5\)
assertion, and no negative complete margin or campaign counterexample is
obtained.
