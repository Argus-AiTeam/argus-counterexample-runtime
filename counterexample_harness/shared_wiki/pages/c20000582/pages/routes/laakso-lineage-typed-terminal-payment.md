---
title: Depth-shifted Laakso lineage-typed terminal payment
description: Exact local closure by an address-independent reflected table that pays inherited direct S and X debts from direct D and root D.
---

For the canonical max-child margin, retain the initial root \(S,D\) channels
and split every direct packet by its \(S,X,D\) origin, birth digit, and age.
At the terminal clock, freeze one rational same-clock table indexed only by
the last generator, candidate child, clock, source lineage, and target
lineage.  Only direct lineages may receive payments; inherited direct targets
are explicitly allowed.  Reflection complements every digit and preserves
the table.

One 24-entry table with eight nonzero payments closes all four roots and the
reachable \(w=1,j=0\) obstruction, as well as its reflected case.  At
\(w=1,j=0\), inherited direct \(D\) sends \(4/125\) to inherited direct
\(S\) and \(5700000000000000001/10^{20}\) to inherited direct \(X\).
Root \(D\) sends \(1999999999999999999/10^{20}\) to inherited direct \(X\)
and \(10^{-20}\) to the current candidate-direct packet.  Exact
componentwise inequalities leave every donor and target residual
nonnegative; no aggregate suffix or complete margin is used as a capacity.

The complete source reconstruction, payment matrices, residual vectors,
vertexwise conservation equations, and reflection checks are independently
rerunnable.  A typed Jacobian exact LP verifies the two donor budgets; larger
exact encodings hit documented adapter admission bounds and yield no
mathematical conclusion.

This establishes only the frozen local closure lemma.  It does not provide an
all-generator induction and neither proves nor refutes the all-depth
\(q=3/5\) assertion or the counterexample objective.
