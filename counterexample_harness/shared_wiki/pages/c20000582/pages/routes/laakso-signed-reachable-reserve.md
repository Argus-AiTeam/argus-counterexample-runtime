For the complete repeated-digit-\(3\) profiles, set
\[
A(H)=g(H\circ\zeta),\qquad T=6A.
\]
The full five-channel Schur defect admits the exact all-parameter
regularization
\[
A^r(F_k-F_0)
=4\cdot72^8\zeta_r^{-26}\Pi(\zeta_r)
\sum_{i=0}^{k-1}A^{i+r}h ,
\]
where \(h\) and every \(A^ih\) are coefficientwise nonnegative.
The explicit polynomial \(\Pi\) has degree \(24\), constant coefficient
one, and 25 strictly alternating coefficients.  Thus this is a
phase-dependent positive-core reduction, not a fixed-dimensional profile
state, but positivity of the core alone cannot control its localizer.

This failure is exact rather than qualitative.  If \(q=4^r\) and the
first nonzero coefficient of \(H_{k,r}\) is scaled to one, its first three
coefficients are
\[
1,\qquad 16q+\frac12,\qquad 128q^2+14q+\frac13.
\]
Their first Hankel minor is
\[
-128q^2-2q+\frac1{12}<0.
\]
Thus the actual core is neither a positive Hausdorff/Stieltjes
coefficient-moment sequence nor Hankel-total-positive at any phase.  The
dual kernel \(t^{24}\Pi(1/t)\) is positive at \(t=1/10\) and negative at
\(t=1/6\), so abstract moment positivity would not sign even the
same-variable base stencil without quantitative band balance.  This
kernel is not a direct \(z\)-coefficient dual for
\(\Pi(\zeta_r(z))\) when \(r>0\).

The actual \(k=1,r=0\) common-projector measure has 73 algebraic atoms.
Exact Sturm and residue intervals isolate
\[
\lambda_{71}<999/1000<\lambda_{72}
\]
and give a strictly negative top signed weight
\[
-1/16000<w_{72}<-1/17000<0
\]
for \(9\,d\Omega_{FF}-d\Omega_{NN}\).  Hence every nonnegative localizer
supported above \(999/1000\) and nonzero at the top atom has strictly
negative signed integral.  This exhausts arbitrary finite upper-gap
hinge/Abel order and every nonnegative mixture, even when the threshold
and order depend on phase.

All six reviewed cells
\[
(1,0),(1,1),(1,2),(2,0),(2,1),(3,0)
\]
remain exactly positive under complete full-profile and joint-measure
replay, with all clocks \(2\cdot18^j\), contacts, reflections, cross
measure, and old-prefix normalizations retained.  No universal sign or
negative complete contact follows.  Any viable continuation must use
quantitative compensation between the negative top block and lower
spectral regions, or a materially different reachable invariant.
