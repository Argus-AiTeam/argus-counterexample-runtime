# Endpoint-notch positive homogeneous cone exhaustion

For depth \(m\), put
\[
N=\frac{m(m+1)}2,\qquad h=\left\lfloor\frac N2\right\rfloor.
\]
The three pairs
\[
(e,a),\qquad(e,a^{2^h}),\qquad(e,a^{2^{2h}})
\]
belong to the genuine word ball of radius \(R_m=2^N\). Their horizontal
scales \(t_-,t_0,t_+\) satisfy \(t_0^2=t_-t_+\).

Every positive homogeneous endpoint sum restricts horizontally to a
positive power sum
\[
P_m(t)=\sum_jw_{m,j}t^{1-\eta_{m,j}},
\]
so Cauchy--Schwarz forces
\[
P_m(t_0)^2\leq P_m(t_-)P_m(t_+).
\]
By contrast, exact integral bounds for the frozen notch profile give
\[
\frac{H_m(t_0)}
{\sqrt{H_m(t_-)H_m(t_+)}}
\geq\frac{m}{2028\sqrt{24}}>\frac{m}{10140}.
\]
Therefore the all-pair ratio spread of every positive homogeneous sum
against \(\rho_m^2\) exceeds \(m/10140\), and every symmetric
squared-distance comparison constant is at least
\(\sqrt{m/10140}\). The result is independent of the number of atoms and
also applies to positive integral mixtures with finite values at the
three witnesses.

**Scope.** This exhausts positive homogeneous decompositions of the
complete endpoint-notch metrics. It does not obstruct signed sums,
nonlinear constructions, or arbitrary finite-dimensional embeddings, so
the endpoint-notch family remains only a nonhomogeneous candidate.
