# Endpoint-notch growing-depth final-envelope boundary

For the canonical longest final envelope \(F_{m,\epsilon}\), let
\[
J=S_m-(30m+\epsilon+d)
\]
at every eligible even depth \(42\le d\le D_{m,\epsilon}\). The
corresponding original frame scale is
\(k_d=e_0+30m+\epsilon+d+2\). The canonical packet specializes the
reviewed exact map
\((p,g,o,q)\mapsto(x_{o,q},k_d,g)\); its eight orbit nodes remain
eight original directed incidences. It uses the exact endpoint denominator
\(\Phi_m(16T,\pm128p_\perp T)\), retains incomplete incidences as direct
terms, and applies the single normalization \(2K_m|I_m|\) only after
all depths are summed.

Put
\[
B_m=m(2\lceil\log_2m\rceil+21).
\]
When \(J\ge B_m\), the reviewed tie-safe final-envelope comparison
extends uniformly. The exact replay checks every \(h=2^{-J}\)-sensitive
candidate, localization, top-address, and query-motion inequality;
the first nonempty bulk case is \(m=568,d=64\). The actual normalized
contribution of each complete incidence is then below
\(55/(448m^3)\). When \(0\le J<B_m\), the
one-Lipschitz inequality bounds each exact incidence quotient by one;
there are at most \(B_m\) distinct scale values. The complete
final-field period count bounds all incomplete incidences by
\(46\,2^{e_0+S_m-K_m+21}\). Hence, for both parities and every
\(m\ge512\),
\[
{\cal A}_{m,\epsilon}
<\frac{55}{448m^3}
 +\frac{m(2\lceil\log_2m\rceil+21)}{K_m}
 +46\,2^{e_0+S_m-K_m+21}.
\]

On \(m_j=2^j,\epsilon_j=0\), \(j\ge9\), this is bounded by
\[
e_j=\frac{55}{448\,2^{3j}}
 +\frac{4j+42}{2^j}
 +46\,2^{17-2^{2j-3}}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus the canonical direct prefix-gap aggregation route is exhausted,
and its proposed uniform positive floor is impossible. This does not
prove decay of the full \({\mathsf Q}_m\) form: incidences outside the
canonical old-screw packet remain unrestricted. It proves neither
\(\eta_m\to0\), a finite obstruction block, nor the campaign
counterexample.
