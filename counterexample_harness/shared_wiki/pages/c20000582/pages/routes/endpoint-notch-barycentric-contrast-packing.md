---
title: Endpoint-notch positive barycentric contrast boundary
description: A Hilbert variance identity bounds every D=2 positive-gap family by five contrasts, exhausting the all-depth positive-measure packing route.
---

For arbitrary probability pairs \((\mu_p,\nu_p)\) on any endpoint-notch
block, write \(b_p\) for the difference of their barycenters in the exact
source Hilbert realization and \(s_p\) for the midpoint of those
barycenters. The proposed variance gap has the exact form
\[
\Gamma_{pq}^{D}
=\frac14\|b_p-b_q\|^2
-(D^2-1)\left[
\frac{\tau_p+\tau_q}{2}
+\frac12\|s_p-s_q\|^2
+\frac18\|b_p+b_q\|^2
\right],
\]
where \(\tau_p\) is the sum of the two within-measure variances.

At \(D=2\), positivity for every pair forces the normalized \(b_p\) to have
pairwise inner product strictly below \(-1/5\). The squared norm of their
sum then proves \(q\le5\). On the other hand, the self-moment bound and
positive gap imply \(U\ge\gamma\), so the required volume inequality at
\((n,D)=(2,2)\) demands
\[
q>(1+2\sqrt{U/\gamma})^2\ge9.
\]
The contradiction is independent of depth, support size, word budget, and
rational denominators. It exhausts all positive probability-pair
barycentric constructions on this family, but not signed contrasts,
different complete-EDM mechanisms, the endpoint-notch blocks themselves,
or the Lang--Plaut problem.
