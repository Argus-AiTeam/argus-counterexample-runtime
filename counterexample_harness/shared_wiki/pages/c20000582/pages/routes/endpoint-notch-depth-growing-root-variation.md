# Endpoint-notch depth-growing root-variation boundary

For every finitely supported rational root field, extended by zero off
its carrier, the dilated six-cycle factors in the Heisenberg group ring
as
\[
1-A+AB^2-AB^2A+BA^2-B
=(-1-AB^2+B+BA)(A-1).
\]
After the exact left-\(b^t\) root difference, this gives four
right-\(a^t\) complete-pair edge families. If \(y_m\) is formed only
after collecting every repeated frame occurrence, then
\[
\frac{\Gamma_m(y_m)}{\|y_m\|_2}
\leq
\frac{4\sum_j
\|\beta_{m,j}(b^{-t_j}\,\cdot)-\beta_{m,j}\|_1}
{\|y_m\|_2}.
\]
This screen includes all zero-extension boundaries and is valid for
arbitrary dependence on the central root coordinate.

The frozen successor is the nonperiodic rational geometric field
\[
\beta_{m,j}(U,V,\lambda)
=\frac{r_m^{U+V-2L_m}}
{(1-r_m^{-t_j})^2(1-r_m^{-2t_j})}
\mathbf1_{\mathcal Q_m}(U,V,\lambda),
\qquad
r_m=\frac{K_m+1}{K_m}.
\]
Before cutoff, \((K_m+1)\beta_{m,j}\) is an exact one-step
\(V\)-primitive of \(\beta_{m,j}\), so the primitive amplification
grows with depth. On every complete-core point,
\[
C_{t_j}\beta_{m,j}=r_m^{u+v-2L_m}>0,
\]
giving genuine collision support on more than \(Q_m/8\) points at more
than \(K_m/2\) scales.

The exact zero-extended variation and unique upper-right carrier corner
nevertheless give
\[
\frac{\Gamma_m(y_m)}{\|y_m\|_2}
<\frac{K_m+1}{500L_m}<\frac1m\to0.
\]
This excludes only the frozen geometric field. The arbitrary variation
screen need not vanish, so fields with positive normalized
\(V\)-variation and the unrestricted transport-gap dichotomy remain
open. The all-depth derivation and exact replay are under
