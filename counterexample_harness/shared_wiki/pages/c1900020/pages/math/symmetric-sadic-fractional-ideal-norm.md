description: Exact run-block domination reverses the proposed fractional-ideal norm decay on both historical actual rays.
---

For the two actual \(m=4\) aperiodic rays behind the 105- and 226-step
false cubic recognitions, let \(R_k=B_k\) and \(R_k=C_k\), respectively,
at exact \(\tau=\sigma^3\) supertile boundaries. With

\[
h_{k,j}=e_j^TR_k^{-1},\qquad
H_{k,j}=\lVert h_{k,j}\rVert_1,\qquad
L_{k,j}=h_{k,j}(1,y,z)^T,
\]

the canonical fractional-ideal obstruction does not decay. Instead,

\[
H_{k,j}^2L_{k,j}\longrightarrow+\infty
\qquad(j=1,2,3)
\]

for both rays.

At the level-one alphabet, every deep seed-1 prefix is a concatenation
of

\[
02^4,\qquad12^4,\qquad12^5,
\]

and every deep seed-2 prefix has one fixed leading \(2\) followed by the
same run language. This language is closed under \(\tau\), and its number
of run blocks satisfies

\[
n_k\ge21\cdot101^{k-2}
\quad\text{and}\quad
n_k\ge24\cdot101^{k-2}
\]

for the seed-1 and seed-2 rays, respectively.

For each of the three true run-block matrices \(W\), exact integer
adjugate arithmetic gives

\[
J W^{-1}J=\pm G,\qquad J=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1),
\]

with \(G\) strictly positive. If \(f\) is the maximum row sum of \(W\)
and \(g\) is the minimum row sum of \(G\), all six coding/block checks
satisfy

\[
g^2>2f.
\]

The signs therefore telescope over arbitrary run concatenations, giving
an exponential lower bound \(H_{k,j}^2/\lVert R_k\rVert_\infty\).
Exact tail-cone bounds give \(L_{k,j}>1/(4\lVert R_k\rVert_\infty)\)
for the 105-step ray and
\(L_{k,j}>1/(12\lVert R_k\rVert_\infty)\) for the 226-step ray. The
latter ray's one fixed leading matrix contributes only a constant factor
to the combined lower bound for \(H_{k,j}^2L_{k,j}\).

This result retires only the zero-liminf canonical-row mechanism.
It proves neither field nonmembership nor cubicity, and the algebraicity
of both actual aperiodic rays remains unresolved.
