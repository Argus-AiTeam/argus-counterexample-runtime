title: D81 finite anchored two-shear atlas boundary
description: The complete h=21 two-shear cells have no target hit, while no finite fixed-anchor H_i L(v) U(u) atlas covers the full quotient interface.
---

The completed exact \(h=21,j=0\) classification of
\[
H_\delta L(v)U(u),\qquad u,v\ge1,
\]
covers \(76{,}600\) forced envelope points in both determinant
orientations.  Exactly \(48{,}543\) pass every coarse gate, all cross the
target strictly, and none reaches target equality or supplies a true
target-hit seed.  The envelope follows from exact inequalities rather than
an imposed parameter box.

This finite boundary cannot be promoted to a complete quotient atlas.  For
a fixed
\[
H=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in GL_2(\mathbf Z),
\]
the first column of \(HL(v)U(u)\) is
\[
\operatorname{col}_1(H)+v\operatorname{col}_2(H);
\]
it does not depend on \(u\).  The primitive columns
\[
P_n=(n,n^2+1)^t
\]
meet this affine line only when
\[
-bn^2+dn+b(c-1)-ad=0.
\]
This is a nonzero polynomial of degree at most two for every unimodular
\(H\).  Hence a finite list of fixed anchors covers only finitely many
\(P_n\), and no finite anchored two-shear atlas covers
\(GL_2(\mathbf Z)\).

The complete disjoint interface instead retains an arbitrary primitive
first column, determinant orientation, Bezout completion and integer shear,
followed by two unique normalization carries.  At adjacent levels these
parameters remain independent until
\[
W_j=B_j(t_j^{2h}B_{j+1})^{-1}
\]
passes the complete equality-aware true-word test.  That unbounded coupled
domain remains open.  The result retires only finite fixed-anchor transport;
it is neither an all-level D81 exhaustion nor a Jacobi--Perron
counterexample.
