# Geffroy diagonal-divisor transport

Geffroy V6/revision 2.8, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.21992774`, studies witnesses
\(d\mid n\) for which \(d+p\mid n+p\), with \(p\) an odd prime
displacement. This relation is not the Erdos-Selfridge edge
\(q\mid P+1\) between prime vertices.

The source itself makes the mismatch exact. For odd \(n\) in the
prime-level first-return stratum, Theorem 12.6 gives
\(\operatorname{spf}(n)\ge p\). Hence every prime witness \(d\ge p\), so
\(d\nmid p+1\). For even \(n\), Lemma 12.21 makes every witness even; the
only prime possibility is the terminal height-zero prime 2.

Theorem 12.18's positive lower density uses the diagonal witness \(d=p\)
inside composite addresses \(n=pm\). That witness also never divides
\(p+1\). Thus the established \(1/(p\log p)\) first-return density has zero
positive-height target-compatible flow under its source indexing. Reversing
the labels would require the additional condition \(p\mid d+1\), which the
source does not state or count.

This retires only the direct Geffroy transport mechanism. It does not
refute the source density theorem, prove positive exponential exact-level
mass, construct a fixed-exponential exact-class family, or give an Erdos
counterexample.
