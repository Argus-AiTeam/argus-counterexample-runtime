---
title: Odd-prime restricted Heisenberg Hopf-superalgebra boundary
description: Complete Koszul-signed Hopf data and the all-odd-prime scalar-one formula for AK(3).
---

Over \(\mathbb F_p\) for any odd prime \(p\), the superalgebra

\[
H_p=\mathbb F_p\langle x,y,z\rangle/
(x^2,y^2,z^p,[z,x],[z,y],xy+yx-z),
\]

with \(x,y\) odd and \(z\) even, has PBW basis
\(x^a y^b z^c\), \(a,b\in\{0,1\}\), \(0\le c<p\). With all three
generators primitive it is super-cocommutative. The only nontrivial
coproduct sign block is

\[
\Delta(xy)=xy\otimes1+x\otimes y-y\otimes x+1\otimes xy.
\]

The even element \(L=xyz^{p-1}\) is a two-sided integral, and extraction of
its PBW coefficient is an even two-sided cointegral \(l\), normalized by
\(l(L)=1\).

For Bobtcheva's presentation morphism in
\(\mathrm{sVect}_{\mathbb F_p}\),

\[
\overline\Omega_{H_p}(\operatorname{AK}(3))
=1
=\overline\Omega_{H_p}(\langle a,b\mid a,b\rangle)
\]

for every odd prime \(p\). This is not a finite-prime extrapolation. The
dual coordinate superalgebra is finite local Frobenius, and its tensor
square has one-dimensional socle generated by
\(\delta\otimes\delta\), where \(\delta=l\). The AK(3) word map has
cotangent matrix

\[
\begin{pmatrix}3&-4\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}
\]

of determinant one. Comparing it with an explicit Nielsen lift having the
same cotangent map shows that it fixes \(\delta\otimes\delta\), so the
closed scalar is \(\mu(\delta)^2=1\).

At \(p=3\), a full primitive-placement contraction with all Koszul
permutation signs and an independent streamed right-associated contraction
both replay scalar one. The complete formulas, signed tables, and exact
replay are in `research/experiments/hopf-super-heisenberg/`.

This exhausts the stated odd-prime restricted Heisenberg super family only.
It does not identify an Andrews--Curtis counterexample and does not enlarge
the previously exhausted ordinary-Hopf or dual-number classes.
