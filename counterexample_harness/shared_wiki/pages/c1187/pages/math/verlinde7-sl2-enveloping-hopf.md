# Canonical \(U(\mathfrak{sl}_2)\) boundary in
\(\operatorname{Ver}_7^+\)

Newton's highest-weight-indexed \(L_2\) is the campaign's
dimension-indexed \(L_3\).  Transporting his
\(\operatorname{proj}^{2,2}_2\) bracket into the reviewed
\(\operatorname{Ver}_7^+\) basis gives a nonzero antisymmetric,
\(C_7\)-equivariant Lie bracket satisfying Jacobi and categorical
\(7\)-Jacobi.

The internal enveloping quotient has PBW carrier
\[
U(L_3)\cong
\mathbf1+L_3+(\mathbf1+L_5)+L_3+\mathbf1
=3\mathbf1+2L_3+L_5.
\]
Below filtration degree four its generator action comes from ordinary PBW
symmetrization.  At the top boundary the complete categorical Lie relation
uniquely selects coefficients \((5,2)\) for the two channels
\(L_3\otimes S^4(L_3)\to S^1(L_3),S^3(L_3)\).  The resulting action
generates all fourteen carrier dimensions, has Newton's bracket as its
commutator, and has associated graded multiplication \(S(L_3)\).

The primitive coproduct, antipode, unit, and counit pass every complete
trace-quotient Hopf coordinate.  The multiplication has a nonzero
commutativity defect.  The two-sided integral and cointegral spaces are
one-dimensional; in the degree-zero, degree-two, and degree-four unit
summands,
\[
L=-1_{\deg0}-1_{\deg2}+1_{\deg4},
\qquad
\lambda=\text{degree-four projection},
\qquad
\lambda(L)=1.
\]
Thus this is an admissible normalized noncommutative internal Hopf object.

For the literal AK(3) relators
\[
x^3y^{-4},\qquad xyxy^{-1}x^{-1}y^{-1},
\]
a streamed occurrence contraction and an independently ordered
primitive-coproduct PBW-placement tensor-network contraction agree exactly:
\[
\overline\Omega_{U(L_3)}(\mathrm{AK3})
=1
=\overline\Omega_{U(L_3)}(\mathrm{standard}).
\]
The second schedule contracts 37 exact factors by global finite-field
variable elimination and has maximum joint rank five.  The independent
verifier also matches it against the prior sparse placement algorithm on
short exact probes.

Consequently the canonical internal \(U(\mathfrak{sl}_2)\) does not
separate AK(3).  This is an exact boundary only for this object and this
presentation; it does not cover other internal Lie algebras, Hopf objects,
or presentations.
