# Minimal internal Verlinde Hopf boundary on the AK family

For \(H=2\mathbf1\oplus X\) in
\(\operatorname{Ver}_5^+\), the reviewed normalized unimodular
cocommutative Hopf-structure scheme reduces, up to scalar-preserving object
isomorphism, to the two classes \(t=0\) and \(t\ne0\).

For either class, the variable power blocks in

\[
\operatorname{AK}(n)=
\langle a,b\mid
a^n b^{-(n+1)},\
a b a b^{-1}a^{-1}b^{-1}\rangle
\]

are represented by an exact 625-dimensional transfer operator \(T\) over
\(\mathbb F _5\). A fixed boundary functional \(\Lambda\) consumes the
extra negative \(b\)-slot and the six fixed braid-word slots, giving

\[
\overline\Omega_H(\operatorname{AK}(n))=\Lambda(T^n v_0).
\]

Coordinatewise exact computation proves

\[
T^5v_0=v_0.
\]

Two independent occurrence-level contractions give
\((q_0,q_1,q_2,q_3,q_4)=(1,1,1,1,1)\) for each class. Hence

\[
\overline\Omega_H(\operatorname{AK}(n))=1
\]

for every \(n\ge2\), equal to the standard-presentation scalar. This
exhausts only this Hopf object on the AK family; it is not a global
Andrews--Curtis conclusion.
