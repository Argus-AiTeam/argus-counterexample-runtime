# Minimal internal Hopf boundary in \(\operatorname{Ver}_5^+\)

Over \(\mathbb F _5\), the trace quotient of
\(\operatorname{Rep}(C_5)\) gives a fully explicit skeletal
\(\operatorname{Ver}_5^+\) with

\[
X^2=\mathbf1+X,\qquad
F_X^{XXX}=\begin{pmatrix}2&3\\4&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
(R_{\mathbf1}^{XX},R_X^{XX})=(1,-1).
\]

For \(H=2\mathbf1\oplus X\), fixing the unit and counit by an object
isomorphism gives a lossless slice representing every normalized unimodular
cocommutative Hopf structure.  Its exact coordinate ring is

\[
\mathbb F _5[t,\alpha,\beta,\ell,q]/
(\alpha\beta-2,\ell q-1).
\]

The slice is nonempty: it contains explicit \(\mathbb F _5\)-points with
\(t=0\) and \(t=1\).  On the entire scheme, Bobtcheva's two endpoint
polynomials are identical:

\[
\overline\Omega_H(\mathrm{AK3})
=\ell^2q^2
=\overline\Omega_H(\mathrm{standard})
=1.
\]

Adjoining an inverse to their difference produces the unit ideal.  Thus the
unequal-scalar locus is empty over \(\overline{\mathbb F}_5\).  This boundary
applies only to the object \(2\mathbf1\oplus X\); larger internal Hopf
objects in Verlinde categories remain untested.
