# AK(3) depth-three terminal-coupling boundary

For the normalized AK(3) cell with successive bases

\[
G_2=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\1&3\end{pmatrix},\quad
H=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
K_*=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\quad I,
\]

the complete unrestricted \(S_2\) fiber is

\[
\begin{aligned}
f_1&=A^2+(B-1)h-g,\\
f_2&=-B^{-2}-(1+B)(1+B+B^2)
  (1+(B-1)h(1,B)-g(1,B))+(A-1)k,\\
f_3&=g,
\end{aligned}
\]

with arbitrary finite Laurent \(h,g,k\). Exact collection from the
original AK(3) words gives the same two first-relative-grade residuals
in all eight ordered component patterns.

Reduce modulo three and set \(A=B=-1\). With

\[
H=h(-1,-1),\qquad G=g(-1,-1),\qquad K=k(-1,-1),
\]

the two diagonal derivatives force

\[
-1+H-H^2-HG+HK-G-G^2-GK-K=0,
\]

\[
-1+H+H^2-G^2=0.
\]

Their only solutions in \(\mathbb F_3^3\) are
\((1,1,2)\) and \((1,2,0)\). But the normal derivative of the second
residual requires

\[
1-H^2-G+GK=0,
\]

whose value is \(1\) at both candidates. This excludes every finite
Laurent point without a support bound.

The machine certificate additionally records a sparse polynomial
identity expressing \(1\) in the ideal generated by these three
equations together with \(H^3-H\) and \(G^3-G\). Thus the
contradiction has both an algebraic ideal certificate and an
independent 27-point replay.

The full original-word replay reconstructs all three internal and two
terminal relation-cycle columns in all eight patterns. Every reduced
column has zero outer augmentation, while terminal powers act by
Laurent units. Thus those freedoms cannot remove the derivative
obstruction, and the complete \(K_*\) fiber has no \(S_3\) lift.

This is one exact depth-three cell boundary, not an
Andrews--Curtis counterexample and not a statement about other third
bases or longer cells. The full proof and rerunnable certificates are
in
[`THEOREM.md`](../../../../../research/experiments/ak3-depth-three-terminal-coupling/THEOREM.md),
`full-fiber-symbolic.json`, `mod3-derivative-obstruction.json`, and
`escape-cell.json`. Independent Reviewer replay is still required.
