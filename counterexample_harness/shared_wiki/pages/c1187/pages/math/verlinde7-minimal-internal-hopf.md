# Minimal internal Hopf boundary in \(\operatorname{Ver}_7^+\)

Over \(\mathbb F _7\), exact Jordan-chain decompositions in the trace
quotient give
\[
L_3^2=\mathbf1+L_3+L_5,\qquad
L_3L_5=L_3+L_5,\qquad
L_5^2=\mathbf1+L_3.
\]
The last identity comes from
\[
J_5^2=3J_7+J_3+J_1
\]
after discarding exactly the projective \(J_7\) blocks.  All associators,
symmetry maps, and duality maps are derived from these exact decompositions;
23 nonunit associator blocks and 48 pentagon output blocks pass.

For
\[
H=2\mathbf1\oplus L_5,
\]
the lossless standard-unit/counit gauge for every normalized unimodular
cocommutative Hopf structure has coordinate ring
\[
\mathbb F _7[c,d,\ell,q]/(cd-2,\ell q-1).
\]
This scheme is nonempty, for example at
\((c,d,\ell,q)=(1,2,1,1)\).  Residual rescaling of the
\(\mathbf1\)-summand sends every algebra/coalgebra point to \(c=1,d=2\);
normalized integral/cointegral rescaling then sends every geometric point to
the displayed representative without changing a balanced closed scalar.

The extra \(L_3\) fusion channel changes the unreduced AK(3) polynomial but
does not separate it:
\[
\Omega(\mathrm{AK3})
=-(cd)^2\ell^2q^2-cd\,\ell^2q^2
=\ell^2q^2
=\Omega(\mathrm{standard})
=1.
\]
Independent streamed and primitive-placement contractions both reproduce
the value one at the explicit point.  The unequal-locus ideal has basis
\([1]\), with explicit identity
\[
1=-z(cd+3)\ell^2q^2(cd-2)
 -(z(\Omega(\mathrm{AK3})-\Omega(\mathrm{standard}))-1).
\]
Thus this object cannot separate AK(3).  Larger internal objects and other
non-Tannakian categories remain open.
