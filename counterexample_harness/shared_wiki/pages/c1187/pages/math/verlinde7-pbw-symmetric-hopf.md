# Canonical symmetric Hopf boundary in
\(\operatorname{Ver}_7^+\)

For \(X=L_3\) over \(\mathbb F _7\), the canonical symmetric algebra is
finite:
\[
S(X)=\bigoplus_{d=0}^4 S^d(X).
\]
Exact Jordan decompositions in the reviewed trace-quotient model give
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
d&S^d(J_3)&S^d(L_3)\\ \hline
0&J_1&\mathbf1\\
1&J_3&L_3\\
2&J_5+J_1&L_5+\mathbf1\\
3&J_7+J_3&L_3\\
4&2J_7+J_1&\mathbf1\\
5&3J_7&0.
\end{array}
\]
Thus the representative of
\[
S(L_3)=\mathbf1+L_3+(\mathbf1+L_5)+L_3+\mathbf1
\]
has ordinary dimension fourteen.

Polynomial multiplication through degree four, zero products above the
top degree, and the primitive polynomial coproduct descend through the
recorded surviving-block inclusions and projections.  With antipode
\((-1)^d\) in degree \(d\), the degree-four inclusion is a two-sided
integral and the degree-four projection is a two-sided cointegral.  They
satisfy \(\lambda(L)=1\).

Complete trace-quotient coordinates vanish for associativity,
coassociativity, bialgebra compatibility, commutativity, cocommutativity,
both antipode identities, involutivity, both integral identities, and both
cointegral identities.  The full operation tables are therefore an exact
normalized unimodular cocommutative Hopf certificate, not a fusion-ring
ansatz.

For the literal AK(3) relators
\[
x^3y^{-4},\qquad xyxy^{-1}x^{-1}y^{-1},
\]
a streamed occurrence contraction and an independent primitive-placement
block contraction both give
\[
\overline\Omega_{S(L_3)}(\mathrm{AK3})
=1
=\overline\Omega_{S(L_3)}(\mathrm{standard}).
\]
Hence the canonical symmetric object cannot separate AK(3).  This boundary
does not extend to other internal enveloping algebras, other Hopf objects,
or other presentations.
