# Canonical \(U(\mathfrak{sl}_2)\) on the
\(\operatorname{MS}(2,w)\) family

For the reviewed normalized internal enveloping Hopf object
\[
U(L_3)\cong3\mathbf1\oplus2L_3\oplus L_5
\quad\text{in}\quad\operatorname{Ver}_7^+,
\]
Bobtcheva's scalar is exactly one on every
\[
\operatorname{MS}(2,w)=
\langle x,y\mid x^{-1}y^2xy^{-3},xw^{-1}\rangle
\]
with \(w\) freely reduced and \(\exp_x(w)=0\).

The occurrence-faithful transfer state
\((a,u,v)\in H^{\otimes3}\) has dimension \(14^3=2744\).
Its four exact letter operators retain the current noncommutative
relator product and the unconsumed \(x\)- and \(y\)-coproduct tails.
The terminal functional is cointegral times counit times counit.

The dual subspace generated from that terminal functional by all four
letter operators has exact dimension 179.  Quotienting by its
annihilator preserves every scalar after every future word.  The
complete freely reduced reachable quotient has 29 states and 88 edges.
Although its unrestricted scalar histogram is
\[
0:4,\qquad1:9,\qquad2:8,\qquad4:8,
\]
the complete zero-\(x\)-weight grammar
\[
B\to\epsilon\mid y\mid y^{-1}\mid BB
\mid xBx^{-1}\mid x^{-1}Bx
\]
reaches only five states from the initial state, all with scalar one.
The saturated relation has 117 ordered pairs.

An independent verifier reconstructs the observable subspace by a
different elimination schedule, matches all 29 reachable
representatives in the full 2744-coordinate transfer, recomputes the
grammar by explicit set saturation, and agrees with fresh streamed and
primitive-placement tensor-network contractions.

This exhausts only this canonical internal Hopf object on this
Miller--Schupp family.  It is not a global Andrews--Curtis result and
does not constrain other objects, categories, or presentation
families.
