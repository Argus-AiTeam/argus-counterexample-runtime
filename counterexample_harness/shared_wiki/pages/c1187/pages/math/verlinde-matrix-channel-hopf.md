# Matrix-channel internal Hopf boundary in \(\operatorname{Ver}_5^+\)

For

\[
H=2\mathbf1\oplus2X
\]

over \(\mathbb F_5\), the multiplicity-two \(X\)-block permits arbitrary
noncommuting \(2\times2\) multiplication and antipode matrices.  It also
introduces a genuinely new cocommutative channel

\[
X_k\longrightarrow
(X_0\otimes X_1-X_1\otimes X_0)_X,
\]

because \(R_X^{XX}=-1\).  None of these channels is diagonalized or omitted
in the exact search.

After the lossless standard-unit/counit gauge, all structure maps have 40
coordinates.  Every associativity, coassociativity, braided bialgebra,
antipode, involutivity, two-sided integral, two-sided cointegral, and
normalization diagram gives 196 distinct equations.  Their ideal has reduced
Groebner basis \([1]\).

Normalization forces the non-unit integral and cointegral coordinates to be
nonzero.  Rescaling them gives an equivalent 25-variable slice retaining all
\(X\)-multiplicity matrices:

\[
M_L=M_R=uI_2,\quad d_{aX}=vI_2,\quad
L=a-ue,\quad q=w(a^*-ve^*),\quad w(1+uv)=1.
\]

Its 114 axiom equations also have basis \([1]\), and Singular's exact
transformation matrix reconstructs \(1\) as a polynomial combination of
those generators.  Therefore \(2\mathbf1\oplus2X\) supports no normalized
unimodular cocommutative Hopf structure over
\(\overline{\mathbb F}_5\).  Every Bobtcheva endpoint-separation
Rabinowitsch locus over this object is consequently empty.

This boundary is specific to \(2\mathbf1\oplus2X\) in
\(\operatorname{Ver}_5^+\).  It does not constrain larger internal objects
and does not settle the Andrews--Curtis conjecture.
