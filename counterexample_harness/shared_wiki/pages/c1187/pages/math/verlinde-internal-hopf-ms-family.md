# Minimal internal Verlinde Hopf boundary on the MS(2,w) family

For \(H=2\mathbf1\oplus X\) in
\(\operatorname{Ver}_5^+\), the reviewed normalized unimodular
cocommutative Hopf scheme reduces, up to scalar-preserving object
isomorphism, to the two residual classes \(t=0\) and \(t\ne0\).

Use

\[
\operatorname{MS}(2,w)=
\langle x,y\mid x^{-1}y^2xy^{-3},xw^{-1}\rangle,
\]

where \(w\) is freely reduced and \(\exp_x(w)=0\). Both prescribed
length-six sentinels have scalar one in both classes under two independent
exact occurrence-level contractions.

For arbitrary \(w\), a 125-dimensional \(\mathbb F _5\) transfer state
\((p,t_x,t_y)\) records the current relator product and the two retained
coproduct tails. Four exact letter operators represent
\(x,x^{-1},y,y^{-1}\); terminal cointegral and counits recover the original
finite occurrence contraction at every word length.

Adding the last letter to enforce free reduction gives complete reachable
graphs with 21 states for \(t=0\) and 101 states for \(t=1\). Zero total
\(x\)-weight is decided exactly by saturating

\[
B\to\epsilon\mid y\mid y^{-1}\mid BB
\mid xBx^{-1}\mid x^{-1}Bx.
\]

The two saturated relations contain 85 and 2021 state pairs. From the
initial state they reach 5 and 21 states, respectively, and every one has
terminal scalar \(1\). Hence

\[
\overline\Omega_H(\operatorname{MS}(2,w))=1
\]

for every freely reduced \(w\) with \(\exp_x(w)=0\), equal to the standard
presentation scalar. This exhausts only this Hopf object/invariant and this
presentation-family pairing; it is not a global Andrews--Curtis result.
