---
title: Arbitrary rational maps on the explicit transverse orbit
description: Scope and mechanism of the universal non-density boundary for the reviewed fixed cyclic orbit.
---

For the reviewed extension
\[
G_Q=\mathcal O_E(Q-O)^\times,\qquad E:y^2=x^3-2,
\quad Q=(3,5),
\]
and the reviewed Zariski-dense transverse cyclic subgroup
\(\Lambda=\langle g\rangle\), every \(\mathbb Q\)-rational map
\(G_Q\dashrightarrow\mathbb A^2\) defined on all of \(\Lambda\) has
non-dense real image and misses a rational open ball.

The proof splits according to the reviewed real radial factor. With
nonunit drift, David's lower bound for elliptic logarithms implies that
algebraic coefficient functions vary only subexponentially along the
base orbit. Extreme fiber degrees therefore force each rational
coordinate to diverge, decay to zero, or approach a rational function
on the elliptic base. Bounded accumulation is confined to at most two
algebraic curves, and the remaining orbit image is locally finite.
With unit drift, the orbit closure is two compact circle cosets; the
rational map restricts to proper real-meromorphic curve maps, whose
closed images have planar measure zero.

This boundary is specific to the fixed reviewed orbit and maps defined
over \(\mathbb Q\). It does not settle arbitrary transverse
semiabelian subgroups, and it does not cover rational expressions with
arbitrary transcendental real coefficients. The detailed proof and
rerunnable finite checks are in
`research/transverse_semiabelian_rational_map/`.
