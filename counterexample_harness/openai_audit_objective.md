# Source-bound error search

Search the manuscripts listed in MANUSCRIPTS.json at revision `{revision}`.
{prior}

For each manuscript, target an actual load-bearing lemma or inference.
One independently confirmed counterexample satisfying all original hypotheses
is sufficient for a scoped finding; a full-paper proof or review is NOT required.
Do not presume that a paper is correct or incorrect.

Record the exact original assertion and locator, all its hypotheses, the
counterexample or invalid inference, reproducible evidence, alternative readings
and repairs, and the limited downstream impact. A false lemma does not by itself
prove that the main theorem is false. If no flaw is established, report the exact
scope searched and what remains unresolved, not a clean bill of health.

Keep separate findings for each manuscript; do not transfer a result to companions
without checking its application. A failed strengthening, routine citation,
unverified step or toy Lean analogy is not a manuscript refutation.
Use Lean only for substantive relevant mathematics and state its actual scope.
Preserve all previous drafts and credit others' constructions.

Write the report and verdict paths listed in MANUSCRIPTS.json. Only the native,
Host-invoked independent Reviewer accepts findings and decides completion.
A resource or source-access stop is incomplete, not evidence of correctness.
